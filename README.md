## About

Providing local IT services since 2009. I started in hands-on systems and network
implementation and grew into enterprise cloud architecture and automation engineering:
infrastructure design, governance enforcement, and scalable platform enablement.

Today I do both. In the enterprise space I build automation that removes manual toil,
codifies governance, and gives teams visibility they did not have before. Locally, I take
on flexible IT work for small and medium businesses, and I teach part-time as an academic
instructor and technology consultant for my alma mater.

Alongside all of it I run a small self-hosted homelab as a testbed for the same patterns,
and open-source the pieces that are broadly useful.

**Full portfolio, case studies, and screenshots: [portfolio.anthonyweb.co.uk](https://portfolio.anthonyweb.co.uk)**
